Privacy Policy

Pagecraft: PDF Editor & Sign · Last updated October 25, 2026

Pagecraft ("the app") is a PDF editor that works entirely on your device.

Data we collect

None. Pagecraft has no account system, no servers, no analytics and no advertising. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

Your documents

PDFs, scans, photos and signatures you use in Pagecraft are processed and stored only on your device, inside the app's own storage. They are never uploaded. You can delete them at any time in the app or by uninstalling it.

Camera and photos

Camera and photo library access are used only when you choose to scan a document, add a photo to a page or turn photos into a PDF. Images stay on your device.

Text recognition and scanning

Text recognition (OCR) and document edge detection run on your device. On Android they use Google ML Kit on-device models, which Google Play services may download to your device; your images are not sent to us.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own privacy policies. Pagecraft only receives confirmation of whether Pro is active.

Children

Pagecraft does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.
