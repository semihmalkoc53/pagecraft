Support

Need help or found a bug? Open an issue and include your device model and app version (Settings → About).

Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded?
No. Pagecraft works fully offline; documents never leave your device.

How do I restore Pro on a new phone?
Open Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.

How do I cancel my subscription?
iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

I forgot the password of a PDF I locked.
Pagecraft never stores passwords, so it cannot recover them. Keep a copy of the original file.
