Edit the text of a PDF

Open a PDF, tap the line you want to change and type. Pagecraft reads the original font size, weight, text color and background, so the new words blend into the page, and edited lines stay editable. New on Pagecraft? Tap Try on a sample on Home to edit a sample invoice in seconds.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change text that is already in a PDF, not only add new text?
Yes. Tap any line of a PDF that contains real text and type over it.

Are my files uploaded?
No. Pagecraft works fully offline; documents never leave your device.

How do I restore Pro on a new phone?
Open Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.

How do I cancel my subscription?
iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

I forgot the password of a PDF I locked.
Pagecraft never stores passwords, so it cannot recover them. Keep a copy of the original file.
